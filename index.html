FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to create an account or log in?

No. Click Start Now and the dashboard opens immediately.

Where is my data stored?

VenXora has no server or database. Your data is saved in your own browser on your device, so each person who uses VenXora has their own private workspace.

Can multiple people use it?

Yes. Anyone can open VenXora and use it on their own device, with their own data.

Can I export my work?

Yes. Tasks and projects can be exported or downloaded as CSV files that open in Excel or Google Sheets.

What happens if I delete something?

It moves to Trash, where you can restore it or delete it permanently.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktops.
